How the answers are worked out

Every answer on this site comes from one analysis of CarDekho's listings. This page explains the measures behind the rankings, so you can judge each reason for yourself.

The data

CarDekho's page for each of 1,399 SUV variants (136 models from 25 brands): its features, specifications and New Delhi prices, as listed on 20 Nov 2026. All prices on this site are ex-showroom prices, because they are listed for every variant and are the same in every city.

CarDekho doesn't list every feature for every car. A feature only counts when CarDekho confirms it, and a car only loses points for a missing feature when CarDekho says it doesn't have it. So no car is punished for a gap in the listing.

Value for money

Each variant has a fair price: what the market usually charges for its set of features. It comes from a price model that learns from the listed variants how much each feature adds to an SUV's price. A car priced below its fair price gives you more for your money. "12% below fair price" means it costs 12% less than its features usually cost. A model's value is the average over its variants in that segment or brand.

The labels follow the model: more than 15% below is Good value, more than 15% above is Overpriced, and anything in between is fair.

Features

"Feature loaded" is measured on a fixed list of 29 comfort and technology features that CarDekho reports for most cars. Each one a car has earns 1 point. Safety equipment is scored separately, below, so it isn't counted twice.

A car also loses 2 points for each of these basics it lacks: power steering, air conditioning, power windows, central locking, remote keyless entry, Bluetooth, a touchscreen, a height-adjustable driver seat, rear parking sensors.

Modern, eye-catching features

Features that impress in the showroom but don't change how safe or capable a car is. The modern score is how many of these 13 a car has:

  • a touchscreen of 10.25 inches or more
  • a digital cluster of 10.25 inches or more
  • a panoramic sunroof
  • ambient lighting
  • ventilated seats
  • wireless phone charging
  • wireless Android Auto / Apple CarPlay
  • a 360° camera
  • a powered tailgate
  • premium sound
  • powered front seats
  • a head-up display
  • connected-car features

Engine performance

Power (bhp) and torque (Nm), each compared with the other cars in the same segment or brand: 0 for the weakest there, 100 for the strongest. The engine score is the average of the two. "Strong" depends on what a car is up against: 150 bhp is a lot under ₹15 lakh, but little above ₹1 crore.

"Modern features but a weak engine" means more modern features than the segment's middle car, and an engine weaker than at least half the segment. The reverse question uses the reverse test.

Safety

Gearbox, drive, seats and ADAS

  • Gearbox type is CarDekho's. When CarDekho only says "automatic", the variant name often tells: "AMT", "DCT", "DCA" (Tata's dual-clutch), "CVT" or "IVT" (Hyundai's CVT). A plain "AT" takes the type of the same model's other "AT" variants. The rest are shown as "type not listed". Electric cars have no gearbox, so they are left out of the automatic questions.
  • 4x4 and AWD use the makers' own labels from CarDekho's "drive type".
  • 7-seater means 7 or more seats.
  • ADAS means CarDekho lists at least one driver-assistance feature.

Short lists

When fewer cars qualify than a question asks for (say, a "top 10" for a brand with 4 variants), only the best one is shown, with a note saying so.
