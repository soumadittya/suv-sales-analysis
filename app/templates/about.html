About the data

SUV Value Guide is a small tool for people buying an SUV in India, and for people who review them. It answers three questions: which variants have what I need, is this price fair, and which trim gives the most for the money.

It's an independent project, not linked to CarDekho or any carmaker.

Where the numbers come from

  • CarDekho's pages for {{ "{:,}".format(counts.variants) }} SUV variants of {{ counts.models }} models from {{ counts.brands }} brands.
  • Ex-showroom and on-road prices in {{ counts.city }}, all collected on {{ counts.price_date }}.
  • Each variant's full spec sheet, as CarDekho lists it.

How the sweet spot works

The trims of one model, with the same fuel, gearbox and engine, are lined up from cheapest to dearest. Each step up is valued by what its added features usually cost between trims of the same model.

The {{ sweet() }} is the trim where the value gained since the cheapest trim, minus the extra money paid, is largest.

What it can't tell you

  • These are list prices. Dealers often sell for less, and prices change.
  • Prices are for {{ counts.city }} only. Other cities can differ.
  • "Not listed" means CarDekho doesn't list a feature. It doesn't mean the car lacks it.
  • Feature values are averages across many cars: a guide, not a quote.

The research
